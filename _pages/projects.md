## Tensor networks and quantum-inspired algorithms

I develop and study efficient tensor-network methods for representing and optimizing high-dimensional quantum systems. My current work focuses on tensor rings, low-rank environments, Quantics Tensor Trains, and their applications to computational quantum physics.

## Quantum many-body systems

I am interested in equilibrium and driven quantum many-body systems, especially computational approaches that expose their collective response and transport properties.

## Circuit quantum electrodynamics and readout

My doctoral research addressed microwave-resonator readout of quantum devices, from semiconductor charge qubits to interacting many-body systems. This work combines response theory, Floquet methods, and mesoscopic physics to understand longitudinal and dispersive readout mechanisms.

I am a postdoctoral researcher in computational quantum physics at **TU Wien**, working with Dr. Anna Kauch and Dr. Markus Wallerberger. My current research explores quantum-inspired tensor-network algorithms, with a particular interest in efficient representations and optimization methods for quantum many-body systems.

I received my PhD in Theoretical Physics from **CEA Grenoble**, where I worked with Dr. Michele Filippone and Dr. Yann-Michel Niquet on readout and spectroscopy in circuit quantum electrodynamics, from single-qubit devices to interacting many-body systems. During my PhD, I also spent a visiting period at TU Wien studying the application of Quantics Tensor Trains to one-dimensional spin chains.

My broader interests include **tensor networks**, **quantum many-body physics**, **computational physics**, **circuit QED**, **numerical methods**, and **quantum-device readout**.
